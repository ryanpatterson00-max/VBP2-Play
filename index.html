The game (E-061, E-062): Dev Mode's title screen → Start opens this page as the game: character select (each player picks a profile or a guest; controllers join with A), then a stage. Block 1 has a story intro, four waves and the boss, then an outro and a results screen ranked by hits on the beat and saved to each profile. Pause anywhere with Start, Esc or P. Opened from Dev Mode's lineup (or on its own), the page is this developer sandbox instead.

    
Muffle, foreman of the Hush Crew (the Block 1 boss, or pick him for E1 or E2 to practice): heavy like the cyborg (can't be grabbed, armored while winding up, wide open after a swing). A haymaker up close and a shoulder charge from range. Every fourth swing is MUTE (a dashed ring warns you): a stomp shockwave that knocks you down unless you're in the air or dodging; guarding braces for 3. It cuts the music for three seconds while the beat keeps going. Below half health he's angry: faster, and MUTE every third swing.

    
Rear strikes: use Flank Setup, then hold back (away from the way you're facing) and press Light, Heavy or Grab. Every stance has its own pair. Light hits behind you without turning: the Muay Thai back elbow, the Shaolin back palm, the Serpent's backward spear hand, the Crane's back beak, the capoeira chapa de costas and rasteira de costas. Most Heavies spin you round to face them as they land: the Philly spinning backfist, the Muay Thai spinning back elbow, the Turning double palm, the Armada, the Rabo de arraia and the Parafuso de costas. Floreio's Light is the Macaco, a back handspring onto them. Golden Crane can parry from behind: tap guard just as a strike from behind lands and you parry it and turn to face them. Holding guard still won't block from behind.

    
Capoeira plays like capoeira now. Ginga never stands still: on every beat you rock back and across the street, and an attack that lands on that step back slips past you (ESQUIVA). Its string is Bênção → Meia-lua → Armada (a spinning kick that hits both sides); Heavy from neutral is the Aú batido, a cartwheel that carries you through the enemy and lands facing them with a kick, so you can keep the string going from the other side. Queixada steps you off the line; Meia-lua de compasso drops under strikes and sweeps the whole circle. Jogo de Chão sweeps knock people down (SWEPT), and the Negativa slides in under attacks. Floreio's Parafuso leaps over low strikes. Grabs still beat every dodge: that's the family's weakness.

    
Co-op, up to four players: plug in more controllers and press any button on one to join as P2, P3 or P4 (P1 keeps the keyboard and touch). Each player has their own fighter, style family (View / Back cycles it), meter and special. On-beat hits play your own instrument: P1 brass, P2 808 bass, P3 lead, P4 drums, so a team in step sounds like a band. Two or more players landing on-beat hits on the same beat is a UNISON and tops up everyone in on it. When every player's meter is full, any special becomes the TEAM ENCORE: everyone poses, and the beams hit half again as hard. No friendly fire; street waves grow with the party; a downed player gets back up as long as a teammate is standing.

    
Touch play (button at the top) is for phones and tablets without a controller: the arena goes full screen with a thumbstick that appears wherever your left thumb lands, the face buttons (X Light, Y Heavy, A Jump, B Dash), LB Focus, RB Guard, RT Grab, CLR (Clear the Floor) and a Stance swipe on the right, and Special, Music and Pause along the top. Walk the other way to turn around; hold guard to back up facing forward. Fights play in landscape. Connect a controller and they hide. ✕ leaves touch play.

    
Replays: every fight is recorded from the last reset. Watch replay plays it back exactly (the game is deterministic: same inputs, same fight, every time). Copy the code to keep or share a fight, or paste one to watch it.
      

      
      

    
Custom stage: a stage is just data: its length, where the props sit, and which enemies arrive at which checkpoint. Copy a built-in block as a template, change it (by hand or with Claude), paste it back and load it.
      

      
      

    
Street run (button at the top, pick a stage next to it): a scrolling street. Each wave locks the camera where it starts; clear it and GO → appears, walk right and the camera follows the middle of your party (it only moves forward, and nobody can walk off screen) until the next checkpoint brings the next wave. Defeated enemies stay down, you get a little health back between waves, and if everyone's knocked out the run ends. R runs it back. Block 1 has four waves ending with the cyborg; The Underpass has three, ending with two cyborgs. Sandbox is the old test box.

    
Breakables: throw someone through a vendor table and they take 10 extra and the table's gone (bodies flying into chairs send them skidding too). Kick or strike a folding chair or trash can and it slides into whoever's in the way for 10, or staggers the cyborg through his armor. Tables block walking until they're smashed; hit one enough and it breaks.

    
Enemy roster: pick what E1 and E2 are with the two menus under the arena: Sparring partner, Grabber, Spacer, Cyborg, Muffle, or the five from E-066. Mix them up. Every role has more than one look, and everyone in a role fights the same way.

    
New in E-066. The Offbeat (purple &) swings on the "and" between beats, so mashing on the beat gets you hit, and the Ginga's on-beat slip doesn't save you. Parry him on his "and" and he's stunned for a whole bar. The Can Man hangs back and throws cans and bottles. When one flashes, press grab to catch it and throw it yourself, or press guard to send it back (RETURN TO SENDER). Jogo de Chão ducks under every one, a jump clears it, or close the gap and he backs off. The Shhhher stands in a dashed quiet ring: inside it your hits build no style meter and no streak. Take her out first or fight outside the ring. The B-Boy / B-Girl drops low on every beat and high strikes go over: sweeps, Serpent Low and Chão hit him, and so does anything off the beat. The Hype Man's boombox pulses a pink ring: enemies inside move faster and swing straight through your hits. The boombox takes your hits until it breaks, then he's just a guy. The B-Boy and Hype Man are sandbox-only until Block 2.

    
The Grabber (orange GRAB tell) grabs straight through guard, dodges and jumps, and slams you for 15, or 24 if he catches a Golden Crane or Floreio on their risky base. From mid-range he lunges at you. Two answers: keep moving so his grab whiffs (he's OPEN for over a second), or press grab just as he grabs you to break it (GRAB BREAK). He's human, so you can clinch and throw him too.

    
The Spacer fights with a bō staff that outreaches everything you have, Muay Thai most of all. His thrust runs down a narrow lane, so step up or down the street and it misses. Crowd him and he backsteps away, but once you're inside his reach the staff can't hit you. Corner him, dash in, or out-range him with Serpent pokes, long capoeira kicks or the ball.

    
First enemy · the production-model cyborg: pick Cyborg for E1 or E2. He's slow and hits hard (20 damage), and he can't be grabbed. While he winds up and swings, a chrome glint runs down his arms: he's armored, so your hits only chip him and won't stop the swing. Dodge or block it, then hit him while he's OPEN in his long recovery. Or break his armor the fun way: throw E1 into him, or throw the ball at him (STAGGERED).

    
Family weaknesses are live: Golden Crane and Floreio get grabbed off balance (extra damage, long recovery), so watch for orange GRAB tells. Jogo de Chão lets some strikes pass over you (not all). Golden Crane has the widest parry window; Philly Shell keeps its straight-on parry.

    
Beat test: every hit that lands within about a tenth of a second of the beat counts as on beat: a ring and ON BEAT pop on the target, and roughly 80% more style meter. Off-beat hits still fill it, just slower; nothing punishes you. The metronome at the top and the string lights show the beat with the sound off. Swap tracks (N: Rooftop Cypher, Badge Battle, Stadium Crunk, the Beat Lab's 32-bit songs) and the new tempo starts on the next bar; the lights, the idle bounce and the ginga follow it. The meter only fills for now; the screen-clearing special that spends it comes next.

    
The music answers you: with the music on, every on-beat hit plays a hard stab in the track's key, climbing the scale as your streak grows. Three on-beat hits in a row bring in a lead melody; six bring in claps. Miss the beat, or go quiet for two bars, and those layers drop out. Turning is free now too: flick the right stick (or Q / E) between actions, and rear strikes still work without turning.

    
Pick things up: a smashed vendor table leaves two planks. Stand by a plank, chair or trash can (an RT · U prompt shows) and press grab to lift it. Light and Heavy swing it for extra reach and damage until it breaks (a plank lasts five hits, a chair three, a can two), and grab again throws it into whoever's in the way. Get hit and you drop it.

    
Special sound: when time freezes during the special, the music drops out and a cue hits over the beams: a crowd roar, an air horn, a record scratch into a bass drop, or a choir hit in the track's key. Pick it under Special sound in the Beat panel and preview it there. It plays even with the music off; the music comes back when time resumes.

    
Specials: fill the style meter, then press both sticks together (or H). Time freezes, your fighter does their ritual (point to the sky, fist to the ground, or palms together) and their payoff lands (orbital beams, a shockwave, or a swarm of light). You pick both, plus the name, color and sound, in the Fighter Creator. Every special does the same damage: heavy hits on everyone, capped on bosses, never an instant win. In co-op, P2–P4 bring Block Quake, Monarch Swarm and Orbital Encore for now.

    
Combat rules come from the Combat Lab core you and C built (strings, cancels, parry, dash → roll, clinch, throws, ball, Focus), extended for three style families. Defeated actors reset automatically.
